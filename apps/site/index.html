Common Questions

The things people message us about

Is there a joining fee on top of the plan price?

No. The price you see is the price you pay — no registration or admission fee.

Is there a lock-in period?

The monthly plan has no lock-in; cancel any time. Annual plans run for twelve months from the date you join.

Can I freeze my membership if I travel?

Yes. Annual members can freeze for up to 15 days a year — just let the front desk know before you go.

Is personal training included?

Festival Offer members get two free sessions. Beyond that, personal training is a separate add-on — ask at the desk for current rates.
